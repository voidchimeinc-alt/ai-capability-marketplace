# Engineering Rules

1. Preserve the product thesis: decision quality over catalog size.
2. Never invent pricing, benchmarks, or capabilities. Use `Unknown`.
3. Keep the monolith modular; no fashionable infrastructure without need.
4. Server-first UI; minimize client JS.
5. Every AI output is advisory; facts come from structured data.
6. Prefer small reusable components over monoliths.
7. Record irreversible decisions in `DECISIONS.md`.
8. Quality gate before phase complete: `lint`, `typecheck`, `build`, tests.
9. Commit by milestone, not mega-dumps.
10. Founder-operable admin before clever automation.
