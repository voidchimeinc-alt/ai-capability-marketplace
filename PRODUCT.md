# Product — AI Workbench (working name)

## Thesis

An **AI Capability Marketplace** that helps companies move from:

> I have a business problem → I know which AI to use → I know why → I know who can build it → I can get it built.

Optimize for **decision quality**, not directory size.

## Core products (MVP)

1. **AI Atlas** — discover tools, models, agents, platforms
2. **AI Arena** — compare and benchmark capabilities
3. **AI Network** — capability + proof + outcome profiles for AI Builders; project marketplace

## Killer flow

**I have a business problem** → lightweight context → **Your AI Options** → matching builders.

## Non-goals (V1)

No social feed, chat, escrow, SSO, crawlers, proprietary LLMs, or vanity-scale tool catalogs.

## Success signals

10 companies exploring · 5 useful recommendations · 5 builder contacts · 3 projects posted · 1–3 paid implementations.
